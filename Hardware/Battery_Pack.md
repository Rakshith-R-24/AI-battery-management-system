# Battery Pack

<div align="justify">

The prototype battery pack uses four YOUXI ICR18650 lithium-ion cells connected in series to form a 4S1P configuration. The cells are used as the energy source for developing and validating the proposed Battery Management System. Since the cells are connected in series, individual cell voltage monitoring is required to observe cell-to-cell voltage variation and identify potential imbalance during charging and discharging.

Each cell has a nominal voltage of 3.7 V and a capacity of 2600 mAh. With four cells connected in series, the battery pack provides a nominal voltage of 14.8 V and reaches a maximum voltage of 16.8 V when the cells are fully charged.

The battery pack is interfaced with the BMS sensing and protection stages. Individual cell voltages are monitored through voltage-divider sensing circuits, while pack current and cell temperature are monitored using the respective sensing circuits. The measured parameters are used for battery condition monitoring and protection.

</div>
